# My Favorite Movie: 10 Things I Hate About You

## Why I Love It
It's a **funny**, *romantic*, and relatable teen comedy with characters who feel real.

## Main Characters
- Kat Stratford
- Patrick Verona
- Bianca Stratford
- Cameron James

## What I Like Most
1. The chemistry between Kat and Patrick
2. Kat's strong, independent personality
3. The humor and 90s high school vibe
4. The soundtrack

## Quick Facts
| Detail | Info |
|--------|------|
| Genre | Romantic Comedy |
| Year | 1999 |
| Based on | *The Taming of the Shrew* by William Shakespeare |

## My Favorite Scene
The paintball date, because it's when Kat and Patrick start to open up to each other.

A classic that's **still fun to rewatch**, even years later.
